## What's fixed in 8.0.1

- **Windows, Linux: the Z key opens the player's Picture menu** (zoom, Anime enhance, HDR colour fix), as its button
  and Settings said; before, Z only cycled the zoom. **Shift+Z** still cycles the zoom.
- **Anime enhance tells you whether it's working.** The Picture menu now says, for the video you're watching, either
  *"on: 480p sharpened to 1080p for your screen"* or *"on, but this 1080p video already fills your screen (nothing to
  sharpen)"*. Press **E** to turn it on or off directly; the player says the same when you do.

Everything from 8.0.0 is included: the new goat look, YouTube/Twitch/Dailymotion channels in Live TV, channels that say
why they don't play, the Android TV fixes and more extensions on Windows and Linux.
