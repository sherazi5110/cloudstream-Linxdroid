## What's fixed in 7.2.2

- **Android: typing in Search works again.** Since 7.2.0 the search box showed the last search instead of what
  you were typing, so key presses seemed to do nothing. Type, then press Search (or pick a suggestion).
- **New profile pictures:** 15 goats (nine colours, a king, a DJ, a cap, a cowboy, a professor and a party goat)
  and 25 animals. Pick one under Edit profile → Picture; the old characters are still there too.
- **Android TV: nothing cut off any more.** On a TV the top of Home's banner (the title) was hidden under the top
  bar, a title's page opened scrolled down with its poster and title cut off, and the start animation was cropped.
  The banner now grows to fit its title, plot and buttons, a title's page opens at the top, and the start
  animation fits inside the screen.
- **Windows, Linux: more extensions work.** Extensions built with Jetpack Compose (MovieBoxProvider and about 20
  others) failed with *"does not have member field 'int $stable'"*: they load now. Extensions that read JSON with
  Gson (HiAnime, StreamPlay, ShowBox…), use CloudflareKiller or the WebView cookie store get those too. Checked
  against all 86 extensions of the Phisher repository.
- **Android: the start animation always opens the app.** On some devices it stayed on screen until you tapped
  or turned the device. It now ends on its own after a few seconds whatever happens; a tap or any key skips it.

Everything from 7.2.1 is included: Windows keeps your extensions after a restart, Home no longer jumps to the
top, extensions like Eclipsia (repository 759) work on Windows and Linux and play their best link, and Android
has a ⚙ button for extensions' own settings.
