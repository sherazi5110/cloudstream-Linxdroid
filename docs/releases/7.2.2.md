## What's fixed in 7.2.2

- **A new logo, start animation and offline screen, drawn by Syed Ali Hamza.** The rainbow goat is the logo and
  the app icon everywhere (Windows, Linux, Android, Android TV, the website). The start animation is the goat
  chewing its grass, and when there's no internet Home, Search and Live TV show the goat's "offline" pictures
  with *Try again* and a way to your downloads. Settings → Appearance → **Logo** picks the circle's colour:
  the rainbow, or your theme colour (the circle and the inner ears follow the accent colour, live).
- **Android: typing in Search works again.** Since 7.2.0 the search box showed the last search instead of what
  you were typing, so key presses seemed to do nothing. Type, then press Search (or pick a suggestion).
- **New profile pictures:** 15 goats (nine colours, a king, a DJ, a cap, a cowboy, a professor and a party goat)
  and 25 animals. Pick one under Edit profile → Picture; the old characters are still there too.
- **Android TV: nothing cut off any more.** On a TV, Home scrolled up as soon as the remote landed on the banner's
  Play button (Android TV's "keep the focused item a third of the way down" scrolling), so the banner's title went
  under the extension picker and Catalogs buttons. Home now only scrolls as far as needed and shows the whole banner
  whenever it has the focus. Also, the top of Home's banner (the title) was hidden under the top
  bar, a title's page opened scrolled down with its poster and title cut off, and the start animation was cropped.
  The banner now grows to fit its title, plot and buttons, a title's page opens at the top, and the start
  animation fits inside the screen.
- **Windows, Linux: more extensions work.** Extensions built with Jetpack Compose (MovieBoxProvider and about 20
  others) failed with *"does not have member field 'int $stable'"*: they load now. Extensions that read JSON with
  Gson (HiAnime, StreamPlay, ShowBox…), use CloudflareKiller or the WebView cookie store get those too. Checked
  against all 86 extensions of the Phisher repository. Repository 759's LiveSports loads now too (its specially
  drawn match posters fall back to plain pictures on Windows and Linux).
- **Live TV: YouTube, Twitch and Dailymotion channels play.** Playlists like Free-TV list about 130 channels as a
  YouTube, Twitch or Dailymotion channel instead of a stream. On Android they now play inside Stream Goat, full
  screen, in that service's own player (the embed player they offer apps and sites; for a YouTube channel given
  by name the app first looks up which video is live). CH+ / CH− still change channel. On Windows and Linux they
  play in the same official player in a window of its own (Edge or Chrome's app window). A channel that isn't
  live or doesn't allow other apps says so, with a button to open it in YouTube or Twitch instead. Channels the playlist marks as only playing in their
  own country show *Region-locked*, the list's Ⓢ/Ⓖ/Ⓨ marks no longer clutter the names, and a channel that
  doesn't play says why instead of *"Pick another one in Sources"*.
- **Android: the start animation always opens the app.** On some devices it stayed on screen until you tapped
  or turned the device. It now ends on its own after a few seconds whatever happens; a tap or any key skips it.

Everything from 7.2.1 is included: Windows keeps your extensions after a restart, Home no longer jumps to the
top, extensions like Eclipsia (repository 759) work on Windows and Linux and play their best link, and Android
has a ⚙ button for extensions' own settings.
